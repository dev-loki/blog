## In short

This website is static. There are no cookies, no tracking, no analytics or
statistics tools, and no resources loaded from third-party servers (fonts,
scripts and images all live here). I run no access logging myself. The
technically necessary processing of connection data at the hosting provider is
described below.

runs on. This is technically necessary to establish the connection
(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The blog itself does not log or evaluate these requests.

## Local storage in your browser

The site remembers two settings locally in your browser only: the selected
theme (light or dark) and the history of the terminal in the header. This data
never leaves your machine and is not transmitted to me or to third parties. You
can delete it at any time via your browser's storage settings.


## External links

The blog links to external profiles (such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Xing) and other
websites. When you click them you land on the respective third-party site,
whose data processing is governed solely by its own privacy policy.


## Your rights

You have the rights granted by the GDPR: access (Art. 15), rectification
(Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and objection
(Art. 21). Since no personal data is stored on this blog, most of these rights
are effectively moot here. Address questions to the email above. You also have
the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
